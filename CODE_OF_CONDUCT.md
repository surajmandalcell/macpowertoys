# Code of Conduct

MacPowerToys contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, publication of private information, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner through GitHub. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.
