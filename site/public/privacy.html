Privacy policy

MacPowerToys keeps your data on your Mac.

Last updated November 23, 2026

The short version

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • The app has no analytics, ads, tracking, or crash reporting.
  • The app never sends anything to us. We have no server that collects data.
  • We do not sell or share data, because we never get any.

What stays on your Mac

  • Settings, tool history, logs, and transfer state live in your Mac's Application Support and Preferences folders.
  • Text Extractor reads text from the area you select with Apple's Vision framework, on your Mac. It needs Screen Recording only for that.
  • Color Picker keeps the colors you pick on your Mac.

What leaves your Mac, and only when you use it

Each of these goes straight from your Mac to the service named. That service's own privacy policy applies.

  • Check for Updates: when you click it, the app asks GitHub for the latest release. GitHub sees your IP address, like any web request.
  • RSync UI: rclone talks to the storage you add, such as Google Drive or an SSH server. Your logins stay in rclone's config file on your Mac.
  • Portman: connects over SSH to the servers you add, with your own SSH setup.
  • Portman GitHub links: off by default. When on, it sends a local project's repository name and branch to GitHub to find its pull request.
  • Marketplace: downloads catalogs, icons, and apps only from the sources you add.
  • iCloud settings sync: off by default. When on, it syncs a fixed list of preferences through your own iCloud account. It never syncs logins, file paths, history, or logs.

This website

  • The site uses no cookies, analytics, or third-party scripts or fonts.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site. It handles each request and may keep standard logs, such as your IP address, under its own policy.
  • Downloads come from GitHub, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

MacPowerToys is not made for children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its date change too. The full history is on GitHub.

Contact

Questions: surajmandalcell@gmail.com. Security issues: use GitHub's private report form.
